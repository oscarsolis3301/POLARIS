## Why
Section F of the plan: after the start-up savings of section D have landed, a one-point look or ui
change may still spend most of its time in landing ceremony — three child CLI calls in `self_land`,
two more in `wrap`, a board push per step. The quiet-lane tail collapses those when, and only when,
nothing else is in flight, and keeps every gate (worktree, lock, handoff gates, lease, squash, merge,
tag, report, burndown, content check, seal check, finish). It is built only if the measurement says it
is worth it.
