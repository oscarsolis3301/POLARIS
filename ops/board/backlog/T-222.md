## Why
The integration lease today only recognises the process that took it, so land, seal and done have to take and drop it separately and other lanes slip in between. Implement contract safe-landing section 1: int_on also writes a token (holder pid + nonce) in the lease dir and exports POLARIS_INT_TOKEN; re-entry accepts a matching token as well as $$; int_off (and on_die in core.sh) release ONLY in the frame that actually took the lease. Add a small hermetic golden ops/tests/lease-token.cmd: a child process with the token re-enters (rc 0, no wait) and its int_off leaves the lease standing; a process without the token gets the queued rc 3 path (set integration_wait_minutes tiny in the fixture); the taking frame's int_off removes it. Messages and stale-steal behavior stay unchanged. Offer a helper int_mine (true under pid or token) because land/seal/express in integrate.sh decide 'did we already hold it' with their own land_had/seal_had/ex_had pid checks; T-223 switches them over. Regenerate ops/tests/api-kit.expected (line numbers shift).
