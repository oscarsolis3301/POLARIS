## Why
The owner came back after three hours to nothing changed: a job that hung, a builder polling `bg wait`
forever (rc 2 has no end), and a conductor the Stop hook let go. Today `bg` has no budget, no timer and
no reaper, the lease and heavy slot can hold a foreground wait for 10-30 minutes, and nothing re-enters
a stopped run. This card gives every job a budget with a watchdog that kills the whole process tree,
retries once and then answers exit 4 with a park line; adds `pulse` (what a timer-woken session runs),
`preflight` (the mechanical "needs you before I start" checks) and `defer` (park a command for the
close); makes `next` name dead cards and crashed jobs; and, armed, the lease and heavy slot never wait
in the foreground. It also owns the CLI entry script for the whole plan: the loader stops parsing the
0.52 MB self-test on every start (section D), and the help text names every new verb and flag.
`kit/ops/polaris` is in scope by the owner's word (plan walk-away, Owner decisions).

- Owns `kit/ops/polaris`, `kit/ops/KEYS.tsv` and `ops/tests/api-kit.expected` for wave 1 (walk-away § 0) — rows and help for keys/verbs other cards build are written from the contract.
- The kill drill runs on Git Bash here; the POSIX process-group branch is exercised by CI (Linux/macOS) in the release ritual — say so in the handoff.
- assumed: bg reap kills only an rc-less own-cwd job past its budget whose watchdog is dead or overdue >15s; a live watchdog inside its budget owns the job, and a crashed job (dead runner) is not reaped — it stays rc 3 and next names it crashed: (default-safe)
- assumed: pulse done-after-finish = no armed file + .polaris/handover/<sid>/finished (finish rc 0 writes it); parked = <sid>.parked (any *.parked when no sid) or .polaris/run/stalled (default-safe)
- a <dir>/killing marker covers the kill-to-relaunch window: the runner writes no verdict while it exists and bg wait/status/next never read the gap as crashed (_bgwatch_gone)
- tree kill: Git Bash = /proc/<pid>/winpid + taskkill //T //F then kill -9, drilled green here (bg-budget: 4 grandchildren over 2 attempts, none left); POSIX = own process group (set -m) + one ps descendant walk + pkill -P, TERM, 5 s, KILL — exercised by CI (Linux/macOS)
