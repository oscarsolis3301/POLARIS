# POLARIS × Opus 5.5 — built for the model, and a kit that keeps learning

## Context

Anthropic's Opus 5.5 playbook (claude.dev, 2026-09-22), read with its companion posts on Claude-5
context engineering and dynamic workflows, asks for five things:
- the whole task with a finish line and named stop conditions;
- the stops you want named (Opus 5.5 tends to stop to report: "want me to continue?", a summary naming
  its own next step, a menu of options that don't block anything);
- someone other than the author checking the work, citing evidence;
- few, consistent, progressively-loaded instructions (a contradiction costs it deliberation;
  Anthropic cut >80% of Claude Code's own prompt with no loss);
- thinking depth set by **effort**, not by prose.

The owner wants POLARIS to get the most out of Opus 5.5 **and** to improve itself instead of degrading.

**How we know what to change.**
- A read-only audit ran 6 auditors, one skeptic per auditor, a completeness critic and 2 follow-ups:
  83 findings, of which 79 survived and 4 were refuted.
- Harness facts were confirmed from real transcripts.
- Two red-team skeptics then reviewed this plan, and their fixes are folded in.
- I verified the two flagship findings by hand:
  - EVOLVE's owner-approved lessons in another install (`f5c7489`, 26 lines in `ops/roles/PLANNER.md`) were
    deleted by the next `update` (`bc1fb62`).
  - The brain's kickback parser (`knowledge.sh:582`) can never match. It renders "(no reason
    recorded)" for every kickback, and EVOLVE learned a false lesson from that.

**Keep what already works.** `finish`/`qa` make "done" an exit code. The board is a task list that
survives compaction. Phase boundaries are "starting guns". Fan-out uses disjoint ownership. The
conductor re-runs `qa` itself. The kit has no "think hard" scaffolding and never asks the model to
reproduce its reasoning.

**What's wrong, in four clusters.**
1. **The instructions contradict each other**, and Opus 5.5 pays for every contradiction in deliberation:
   - The polaris skill gives three routes and `kit/CLAUDE.md` gives a fourth.
   - PROTOCOL's "follow literally, never guess" contradicts "default the reversible".
   - PROTOCOL still says "THIS repo pins strong=fable".
   - CONDUCTOR says "route every spawn, pass the model".
   - Three texts disagree on `approve`, and EVOLVE disagrees with Invariant 11 on RULES.
   - The RuFlo home CLAUDE.md (in every session and general subagent) says to use Haiku, to stop
     after spawning, and to init a swarm.
2. **Runs stop to report and drift after compaction:**
   - No text names the stop shapes.
   - The Stop hook passes "Want me to continue?" as if it were a real question.
   - A compacted conductor maps `promote` to "go to the tail".
   - `finish` never looks at `backlog/`, so it can celebrate while dependent tasks were never built.
   - DONE/WON'T live only in chat.
   - The budget caps are prose that dies at compaction.
3. **Work grades itself.** Under `landing: self` no second context reads a diff:
   - `audit` has found zero captures since 6.6.
   - `verify:` is never re-proved on base.
   - A new golden is never seen red.
   - The close report buries what needs the human.
4. **The learning loop is broken, starved, erased and accreting:**
   - It is erased by `update` and fed by broken sensors.
   - It never runs on SOLO/express, "the common case". another install has 44 done tasks and 0 EVOLVE runs.
   - It never sees the human's corrections.
   - Its calibration notes only ever grow.
